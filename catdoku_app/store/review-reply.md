Hello App Review team, thank you for reviewing Kitty Queens: Cat Sudoku. A screen recording from a physical iPhone (latest iOS) is attached.

   - The app has no account registration, login, or user-generated content.

2. Purpose and audience: Kitty Queens is a relaxing logic puzzle for adults and families who enjoy short brain teasers (Queens / Star Battle style). The goal is to place one cat in every color region, with one cat per row and column, and no two cats touching. It offers a few minutes of calm, offline puzzle play, e.g. on a commute.

3. How to use: No setup or login. On first launch a short How-to appears. Tap "Play" for Today's Puzzle (one new board per day) or "Start/Continue" for Levels. Choose "Cat" or "Mark" at the bottom and tap squares. "Hint" gives one correct cat after an optional rewarded video. "All levels" on the Home screen shows every level.

4. External services: Google AdMob (banner ads and optional rewarded videos) and Apple In-App Purchase. No backend server, analytics, authentication, or AI services. All puzzles are generated on the device.

5. Regional differences: None. The app works the same in all regions where it is available. English only.

6. Regulated industry / third-party content: Not applicable. All puzzles, art, and the app icon are our own.

7. In-App Purchase: one non-consumable, "Unlock All Levels" (com.soulfulfill.catdoku.unlockall, $1.99). It unlocks all 500 levels and removes banner ads. Levels 1–30 are free; each further pack of 20 levels can also be unlocked for free by watching one rewarded video. To reach the purchase: Home → "All levels" → the box above the first locked pack (Levels 31–50) → "Unlock all · no ads · $1.99". It also appears after clearing Level 30. "Restore purchase" is at the bottom of the Levels screen.
