## John 1:51 (and 1:46) — "You Shall See Heaven Open"  → used for the 1:51 **review draft**
- Official sermon page: **https://gospelinlife.com/sermon/you-shall-see-heaven-open/** — Tim Keller, **20 Dec 2009**, John 1:43–51, 26:08, SKU RS318-02. Codex read the overview: it links the passage to Jacob and heaven opening to the humbled. (Blocked from the cloud session.)
- Transcript: https://clovermedia.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/0b053f058a/attachments/You_Shall_See_Heaven_Open_Transcript.pdf — **third-party hosted, not an official/verified transcript**. 7 pages. Read by Codex **and** fetched and read by the dev session (2026-11-04; kept only in the session scratchpad, not in the repo).
  - PDF pp.1–2, **02:26–05:05**: Genesis 28 — Jacob fleeing after cheating Esau, alone, dreams of a great stair with angels; the vision means God's presence coming into Jacob's life.
  - PDF pp.3–4, **09:07–11:31**: if Jesus had only said "I can show you the gate of heaven / the connection," that would be a prophet's claim; instead he says heaven and earth meet on him — he himself (his life and death) is the connection, so God can come into the lives of people who have messed up their lives.
  - PDF p.4, **11:31–12:52**: Nathanael's "Nazareth?" = disdain for a backwater, unimpressive place; Keller does **not** connect it to the Bethlehem prophecy.
  - Known transcript errors: "Bethesda" for Bethsaida, misspelled names (Nathaniel, Bauchamp, Otter). Timestamps are the transcript's own, **not checked against the official audio**.
- Status label: **transcript read, audio not verified.**
- Corroborating lead only — **NOT inspected** (403): Redeemer 2006 Genesis leader guide "Heaven's Gate", printed p.189 Q7, https://graceb3.s3.amazonaws.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/05/GenesisLG.pdf . Do not cite as read.
