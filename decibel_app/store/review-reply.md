Hello App Review team, thank you for reviewing Glance dB: Decibel Meter. A screen recording from a physical iPhone is attached (also in the App Review Information attachment).

1. Screen recording: attached. It starts at app launch and shows the meter, tapping Start, live measuring, the noise Report, Settings, and pausing/resuming. The app has no account registration, login, user-generated content, or paid content.

2. Purpose and audience: a free sound level (decibel) meter for anyone who wants to know how loud it is — noisy neighbors, restaurants, baby white-noise machines, concerts. It explains each level in plain English ("Like normal conversation") and saves a simple noise report (date, time, average, max, a chart) to share with a landlord or neighbor. Every feature is free.

3. How to use: no setup or login. Tap Start and allow the microphone. Pause / Resume / Reset control the measurement. "Report" shows the report image and shares it with the iOS share sheet. Tap the level name under the gauge for everyday comparisons. History (clock icon, top left) lists past measurements. Settings (top right): calibration offset, dBA / dBC / dBZ weighting, keep screen on.

4. External services: Google AdMob banner ads only. No backend server, analytics, authentication, payment, or AI services. Measuring works offline. The microphone is used only while the app is open to compute the level on the device; audio is never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere.

5. Regional differences: none. The app works the same in all regions where it is available. English only.

6. Regulated industry / third-party content: not applicable. The app says in the app and in the description that phone microphones are not certified sound level meters and readings are estimates; it makes no legal or medical claims. Hearing-safety tips cite the public CDC/NIOSH 85 dBA guideline as general information. All art and the app icon are our own.
