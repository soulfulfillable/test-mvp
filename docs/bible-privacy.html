Bible Reading Plan — Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

Bible Reading Plan ("the app") helps you follow a Bible reading plan and check off each day's chapters. The app has no accounts, the developer does not collect your personal information, and your reading progress is never sent to the developer. The app shows Google AdMob banner ads on the Map and Plan screens only, described under "Advertising" below. The app does not contain Bible text.

What the app stores on your device

  • The plan you chose (which books, length, order, start date)
  • Which days you checked off and the date you checked each one
  • Your reminder setting and time

This information stays only on your iPhone. Like other app data, it is included in your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backup if you have that turned on. It is deleted when you delete the app. You can export it as a CSV file at any time (Plan → Export Progress).

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder and allow notifications, reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone. No server is involved and nothing is sent anywhere.

Files you export

When you export, the app creates a CSV file and hands it to the iOS share sheet; you choose where it goes (Files, Mail, AirDrop…).

Network use

Plans and progress work fully offline. The app connects to the internet only to load ads (below). It does not use your location.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Map and Plan screens show a banner ad provided by Google LLC through AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS), IP address, and device and app information. This data is processed by Google, not by the developer. See the Google advertising policies and the Google Privacy Policy.

The app does not ask for tracking permission, so your advertising identifier is not shared and non-personalized ads are shown. Ads are limited to general-audience content. No ads are shown on the Today screen.

What the developer does not collect

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers or other contact information
  • Your reading plan or progress
  • Location
  • Analytics or usage statistics (the app contains no analytics SDK)

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
