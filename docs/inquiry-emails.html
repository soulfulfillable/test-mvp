Question about using WCA public data in a free third-party iOS app
Hello WCA Software Team,

I am an independent developer (publishing as Soulfulfill) and a fan of speedcubing. I would like to build a small, free iOS app that helps competitors and their families follow WCA competitions, and I want to make sure I do it in a way the WCA is comfortable with before I write any code.

What the app would do:
- Show upcoming WCA competitions near the user, with dates, events and a link to the official competition page.
- Let a user follow a competitor by WCA ID and see their official results and personal records.
- A simple "for parents" view that explains, in plain language, which round their child is in and whether they advanced.

Data I would use:
- Only public data: the official results export and the public competition listings.
- No login, no private data, and no data on anyone beyond what the WCA already publishes.

My questions:
1. Is an app like this acceptable under the current terms for third-party use of WCA data?
2. The app would be free to use and supported by a small banner ad (no subscription, no paywall). Is ad-supported use permitted? If not, I would like to know before I start.
3. Is there a preferred way to access upcoming competitions and results (the results export, the v0 API, or the unofficial REST API), and are there rate limits I should respect?
4. Is there any approved way to read live results from WCA Live, or should a third-party app only link out to it?
5. What attribution and "not affiliated with the WCA" wording would you like me to display?

I am happy to follow whatever conditions you set, to show the app to you before release, and to remove anything you ask me to.

Thank you for your time, and for keeping this data open.

Best regards,
Soulfulfill
