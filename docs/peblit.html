Peblit

Calm, long videos for sleep, study and reading. Rain, soft piano and quiet rooms.

Every sound on Peblit is played by a generative music system we designed. Nothing is looped and there is no stock music, so every video is a new piece. Each video shows its seed number, so you can come back to the exact same piece.

Watch on YouTube: youtube.com/@peblit

Peblit Uploader

Peblit Uploader is a small private tool that publishes Peblit videos to our own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to other users. Privacy policy
