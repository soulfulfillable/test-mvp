Pop & Topple

Pull a corn kernel back, drop it into a gap, and let the popcorn do the shoving. Five hand-made levels. Phone portrait or desktop.

Play the prototype
  • Pull anywhere on the screen and release to shoot. The dotted line shows the start of the flight.
  • The kernel shakes, then pops. Whatever it is touching gets pushed.
  • Knock the marked bucket down onto the table. Three or four seeds per level, instant retry.
