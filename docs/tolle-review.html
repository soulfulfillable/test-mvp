② 답장 글 (영어 그대로 붙여넣기)

Hello App Review team, thank you for reviewing Tolle: Bible Reading Plan. A screen recording from a physical iPhone running the latest iOS is attached (also added to the App Review Information attachment).

1. Screen recording: attached. It starts at app launch and shows today's reading, tapping Mark as Read (the chapter map fills in), the Map tab (opening Genesis and checking off a chapter), and the Plan tab (plan, length, order, start date, reminder). The app has no account registration, login, user-generated content, or paid content.

2. Purpose and audience: a quiet Bible reading plan tracker for Christians who want to read the whole Bible (or the New Testament, or Psalms & Proverbs) on a schedule, such as in one year. The problem it solves: people lose track of where they are and give up when they fall behind. Tolle shows one reading per day (for example "Genesis 1–3, about 10 min"), lets the user check it off with one tap, fills a map of all 1,189 chapters, and when the user falls behind it offers to spread the remaining chapters or continue from today, without guilt or streak pressure. Users can also check off chapters they read on their own. The app does not contain Bible text; users read in their own Bible.

3. How to use: no setup, login, or sample files. Open the app and today's reading is shown. Today tab: Mark as Read, Undo, Read Ahead, and Adjust Schedule when behind. Map tab: tap a book to check off chapters. Plan tab: choose Whole Bible, New Testament, or Psalms & Proverbs; length (for example 1 year or 90 days); Bible or chronological order; start date; an optional daily reminder (local notification, permission is asked only when the user turns it on); export progress as a CSV file.

4. External services: Google AdMob banner ads on the Map and Plan screens only (no ads on the Today screen, no interstitial or video ads, content rating limited to general audiences). No backend server, analytics, authentication, payment, or AI services. All plans and progress are calculated and stored on the device and work offline.

5. Regional differences: none. The app works the same in all regions where it is available. English only.

6. Regulated industry / third-party content: not applicable. The app shows only Bible book names and chapter numbers, not Bible text. Chapter and verse counts come from an open-source (MIT-licensed) dataset; the chronological order is our own arrangement and is described in the app as approximate. The app icon and all artwork are our own.

Thank you!
