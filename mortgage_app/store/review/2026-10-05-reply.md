Thank you for reviewing Glance: Mortgage Calculator. Here is the information you requested.

1. Screen recording
Attached is a screen recording captured on a physical iPhone running the latest iOS. It starts from launching the app on the Home Screen and shows the typical flow: entering a home price, down payment, interest rate and loan term; opening taxes, insurance, HOA and PMI; adding an extra monthly payment; opening the amortization schedule; and switching to the auto and personal loan calculators.
The app has no account registration, no login, no user-generated content, and no paid content or in-app purchases.

2. Purpose and target audience
Glance is a loan payment calculator for people in the United States who are buying a home or a car or taking out a personal loan. It answers "what will my monthly payment be?" including property tax, home insurance, HOA dues and PMI, and shows how much sooner the loan is paid off and how much interest is saved with extra payments. Every number is calculated on the device from what the user types.

3. How to use the main features (no login, credentials, or sample files needed)
- Mortgage tab: edit Home price, Down payment ($ or %), Interest rate, Loan term and First payment. The monthly payment at the top updates as you type.
- Tap "Taxes, insurance & fees" to edit property tax, home insurance, HOA dues and PMI.
- In "Pay extra", tap +$100 or +$200 (or type an amount) to see the new payoff date and interest saved.
- Tap "Amortization schedule" to see every payment by year or by month.
- Switch to Auto or Personal at the top for car and personal loans.

4. External services
- Advertising: Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) shows one banner ad at the bottom of the screen.
- The app makes no other network requests. It uses no authentication, payment, analytics, or AI services, and has no backend server.

5. Regional differences
The app is designed for U.S. loans (dollars, U.S. property tax and PMI rules) and works the same in every region where it is available. All content is in English.

6. Regulated industry / third-party material
The app is a calculator only. It does not offer loans, connect to lenders, collect financial or personal information, or show live interest rates; the default interest rates are labeled as examples for the user to edit. The app states that results are estimates and not financial advice. It contains no third-party content.

7. In-app purchases
None.
